Dances

Brooklyn Transfer

with Maia McCormick

Becket (CW)

A1 Slide left (2)
Circle left 3/4 (6)
Neighbor swing (8)

A2 Long lines forward and back (8)
Robins chain (to Partner) (8)

B1 Left hand star 1.0 (to long wave with Shadow) (8)
Balance wave, slide right (past Shadow) (8)

B2 Balance wave, slide left (past Shadow, to Partner) (8)
Partner swing (8)

Notes: Written in collaboration with Maia McCormick, riffing on Dancing on the Train, which was riffing on Train Delay.

Dancing on the Train

Becket (CW)

A1 Slide left (2)
Circle left 3/4 (6)
Neighbor swing (8)

A2 Robins chain (to Partner) (8)
Left hand star 1.0 (to long wave with Shadow) (8)

B1 Balance wave, slide right (past Shadow) (8) Balance wave, slide left (past Shadow, to Partner) (8)

B2 Partner balance and swing (8)

Notes: Inspired by Train Delay by Maia McCormick.

Forgotten Dream

Improper

A1 Neighbor right shoulder round (8)
Neighbor swing (face down) (8)

A2 Down the hall four-in-line, turn as couples (8)
Come up the hall, bend the line (8)

B1 Circle left 3/4 (6)
Partner swing (10)

B2 Robins chain (to neighbor) (8)
Mad robin (counterclockwise, around neighbor, to new neighbor) (8)

Notes: Inspired by Forgotten Treasure by Beth Parkes.

Let's Play Already

Improper

A1 Neighbor balance and swing (16)

A2 Circle left 3/4 (7)
Pass through (Partner by right) along the line (2)
Shadow allemande left 1.0 (7)

B1 Partner balance and swing (16)

B2 Long lines forward and back (8)
Robins dosido 1.5 (to new neighbor) (8)

Notes: Written to have as many clear opportunities to role swap and play (partner swap with shadow) as possible.

Star Fleet

Becket (CW)

A1 Long lines forward and back (8)
Left hands-across star 1.0 (8)

A2 Whole set single file promenade (counterclockwise) to new neighbors (N2) (4)
Left hands-across star 1.0 (8)
Larks pull by left while Robins turn alone (over right shoulder) (4)

B1 Half hey, Neighbor (N2 start passing right) (8) Neighbor (N2) swing (8)

B2 Circle left 3/4 (6)
Partner swing (10)

Notes: Inspired by my own dance Strange New Worlds, tightening up its loose timing in B1.

Strange New Worlds

Becket (CW)

A1 Right and left through (8)
Left hands-across star 1.0 (8)

A2 Whole set single file promenade (counterclockwise) to new neighbors (N2) (4)
Left hands-across star 1.0 (8)
Larks pull by left while Robins turn alone (over right shoulder) (4)

B1 Neighbor (N2) right shoulder round and swing (16)

B2 Circle left 3/4 (6)
Partner swing (10)

Notes: Inspired by Star Trek by Mike Richardson. Loose timing around the pull by, sometimes leading to the right shoulder round "in B1" completing before B1 starts.
